About · 关于我

在减法中寻找最有力的表达 Finding strength through subtraction

视觉传达设计师，晋中学院统招本科，专业排名前 15%。扎根山西长治，专注于把地域文化的质感翻译成当代视觉语言。 Visual communication designer, undergraduate at Jinzhong University, ranked in the top 15% of my class. Based in Changzhi, Shanxi — I translate the texture of regional culture into a contemporary visual language.

我是一名视觉传达设计师，专注于品牌识别系统、广告策划与书籍装帧设计。在设计中，我追求「传统韵味 × 当代极简」的融合——从山西地域文化中汲取灵感，用当代设计语言重新诠释。 I am a visual communication designer focused on brand identity systems, advertising and book design. My practice pursues a fusion of regional character and contemporary minimalism — taking cues from Shanxi's material culture and reinterpreting them in a modern visual language.

我熟练运用 Photoshop、Illustrator、InDesign 等传统设计工具，同时积极将 ChatGPT、Gemini 等生成式 AI 融入创作流程，把提案效率提升 35%，创意落地周期缩短 30%。工具在变，判断力不变——AI 负责扩大搜索范围，审美与取舍仍然由设计师完成。 I work fluently in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign, and actively embed generative AI tools such as ChatGPT and Gemini into my process — lifting proposal efficiency by 35% and shortening the concept-to-delivery cycle by 30%. The tools change; judgement does not. AI widens the search space, while taste and selection remain the designer's job.

设计对我来说，是在减法中找到最有力的表达。 For me, design is about finding the most powerful expression through subtraction.
