How it works

合作流程How We Work

01

需求沟通Brief

了解品牌背景、目标人群、使用场景与时间节点，确认设计目标与交付清单。Understanding brand background, audience, use cases and timeline to agree on goals and deliverables.

02

调研与概念Research & Concept

桌面调研与竞品分析，产出 2–3 个方向的视觉概念，附 AI 辅助生成的参考与草图。Desk research and competitive review, delivering 2–3 visual directions with AI-assisted references and sketches.

03

深化与定稿Refinement

确定方向后深化细节，输出规范化的源文件与设计规范，预留两轮修改。Deepening the chosen direction into organised source files and guidelines, with two rounds of revisions included.

04

交付与落地Delivery

交付可印刷／可上线的完整文件包，并配合印厂或开发方完成落地校色与验收。Delivering print-ready and production-ready files, with support through prepress colour checks and final acceptance.
