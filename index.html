02 / About

关于我 About Me

完整履历Full profile →

我是一名视觉传达设计师，专注于品牌识别系统、广告策划与书籍装帧设计。在设计中，我追求「传统韵味 × 当代极简」的融合——从山西地域文化中汲取灵感，用当代设计语言重新诠释。 I am a visual communication designer focused on brand identity systems, advertising and book design. My practice pursues a fusion of regional character and contemporary minimalism — taking cues from Shanxi's material culture and reinterpreting them in a modern visual language.

设计对我来说，是在减法中找到最有力的表达。 For me, design is about finding the most powerful expression through subtraction.
