06 — 信息设计Information Design

左权民歌
发展历程信息可视化 Zuoquan Folk Songs
Historical Infographic

把国家级非物质文化遗产「左权民歌」的发展历程，整理为一张仿古卷轴式的信息可视化长图。 An infographic that reorganises the history of Zuoquan folk song — a national-level intangible cultural heritage — into a single scroll-form long image.

Year

2024

Role

主创设计Designer

Client

课程课题 · 信息设计

Scope

资料梳理 · 时间轴结构 · 谱系分类 · 视觉锚点Research · Timeline · Taxonomy · Visual Anchors

01 / 背景Context

分散的公开资料Scattered sources

左权民歌的脉络横跨数百年，包含曲调分类、代表曲目与几代传承人，但公开资料分散在论文、地方志与口述记录中，缺少统一的视觉秩序。 Zuoquan folk song spans several centuries, with tune families, representative works and several generations of inheritors — but the sources are scattered across papers, local gazetteers and oral records, with no shared visual order.

02 / 策略Strategy

用一条线串起全部信息One line through everything

用仿古卷轴的纵向结构承载时间轴：年代作为主轴，曲调类型、代表作品与人物图像分层挂在轴上，让信息密度很高的内容仍然可以顺着一条线读下来。 A vertical scroll structure carries the timeline: the chronology is the spine, with tune families, representative works and portrait images hung off it in layers, so dense content can still be read along a single line.

视觉上采用戏曲纹样与纸质肌理，让形式本身指向「民歌」的地域属性。 Opera motifs and paper texture push the form itself toward the regional character of folk song.

03 / 执行Execution

先分类，再排布Classify, then lay out

先梳理发展脉络与曲调谱系，建立可扩展的时间轴网格；再排布文字块、数据饼图与人物图像，用统一的对齐线控制长图的阅读节奏。 The history and tune taxonomy were mapped first into an extensible timeline grid; text blocks, data pie charts and portrait images were then laid out against a shared set of alignment lines to pace the long read.

04 / 成果Outcome

一张读得完的长图A long image you can finish

交付一张完整的信息可视化长图，覆盖左权民歌的发展阶段、曲调分类与代表作品。 A single complete infographic covering the development stages, tune classifications and representative works of Zuoquan folk song.
