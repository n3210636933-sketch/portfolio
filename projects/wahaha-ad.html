02 — 广告策划Advertising

娃哈哈 30 周年
广告策划设计 Wahaha 30th
Anniversary Campaign

一份 30 页的完整广告策划案：从市场分析、广告策略，到表现策略与媒介排期，并落地主视觉、IP 形象与平面广告稿。 A complete 30-page campaign plan — from market analysis and advertising strategy through to creative execution and media scheduling, delivered with key visual, IP character and press layouts.

01 / 背景Context

国民符号的当代处境A national icon, today

AD 钙奶是几代人的童年符号，但在今天的饮料货架上，它面对的是新品牌在包装、联名与社交传播上的高频竞争。 AD Calcium Milk is a childhood symbol for several generations, but on today's shelf it competes with new brands shipping packaging, collaborations and social campaigns at high frequency.

30 周年是一次把「童年记忆」重新翻译给新一代消费者的机会，也是把一次性怀旧转化为可持续品牌资产的机会。 The 30th anniversary was a chance to re-translate "childhood memory" for a new generation of drinkers — and to turn a one-off nostalgia moment into a durable brand asset.

02 / 策略Strategy

从「怀旧」转向「快乐」From nostalgia to joy

策划把品牌资产的重心从「怀旧」移到「快乐」，提出广告语「快乐没有保质期」。这句话同时承接老用户的记忆与新用户的好感——它不依赖任何一代人的语境，因此可以被反复使用。 The plan shifts the weight of the brand asset from nostalgia to joy, with the line "happiness has no expiry date". It carries both the memory of long-time drinkers and the goodwill of new ones — and depends on no single generation's context, so it can be reused.

配合广告语建立 IP 形象与包装延展，让「快乐」有具体的面孔，而不只是一句文案。 An IP character and packaging extensions give that idea a face, so "joy" is not only a sentence.

03 / 执行Execution

五段式策划结构A five-part structure

策划案按五段推进：01 市场分析（行业 / 消费者 / 产品 / 竞品）→ 02 广告策略（目标市场 / 消费者洞察 / 品牌定位 / 品牌核心价值 / SWOT）→ 03 广告表现策略（广告语设计 / IP 设计 / 包装设计 / 平面广告设计）→ 04 广告媒介策略（媒介组合 / 媒介排期 / 线上线下传播 / 电商平台）→ 05 结束与附录。 The plan runs in five parts: 01 market analysis (industry, consumer, product, competitors); 02 advertising strategy (target market, consumer insight, positioning, core value, SWOT); 03 creative strategy (tagline, IP design, packaging, press advertising); 04 media strategy (media mix, scheduling, online/offline, e-commerce); 05 conclusion and appendix.

每一段都以可执行的结果收口：策略段落输出定位与核心价值，表现段落输出可直接投产的视觉稿，媒介段落输出预算分配与排期表。 Each part closes on something executable: strategy yields positioning and core value, creative yields production-ready artwork, media yields budget allocation and a schedule.

04 / 成果Outcome

可落地的完整提案A proposal that can ship

完成 30 页策划案，并配套输出主视觉、IP 形象、包装延展与系列平面广告稿，形成一份可直接进入提案与执行的完整方案。 A 30-page plan with accompanying key visual, IP character, packaging extensions and a press campaign — a complete proposal ready to present and execute.
