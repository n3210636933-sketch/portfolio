04 — 书籍装帧 · 插画Book Design · Illustration

《忍道》
书籍装帧设计 "Rendao"
Book Design

以《火影忍者》为题的一本书籍装帧设计，包含封面主视觉、封底信息层级、环衬与漫画式内页版式。 A complete book design on the theme of Naruto — cover artwork, back-cover hierarchy, endpapers and comic-style interior layout.

Year

2025

Role

主创设计Designer

Client

课程课题 · 书籍装帧

Scope

开本与网格 · 封面主视觉 · 封底信息层级 · 内页版式Format & Grid · Cover Art · Back Cover · Interior Layout

01 / 背景Context

把兴趣变成课题Turning an interest into a brief

这是一次把个人兴趣转化为设计课题的练习。用一本结构完整的书，检验开本、网格、字体与图像叙事能不能统一在同一套秩序里。 This was an exercise in turning a personal interest into a brief: using one complete book to test whether format, grid, typography and image narrative can be held inside a single order.

02 / 策略Strategy

文字与图像的对抗Type against image

封面用主角面部特写作为主体，「忍道」两个汉字以极大的字号压在画面上，形成文字与图像互相侵占的关系——字体本身成为构图的一部分，而不是标题。 The cover uses a close-up of the protagonist's face, with the two characters "忍道" set at an extreme size so that type and image invade each other — the lettering becomes part of the composition rather than a caption.

封底则完全相反：大面积留白，只保留一枚圆形标志与出版信息，让翻到书末的动作有一个安静的落点。 The back cover does the opposite: generous white space, a single circular mark and the imprint details, giving the act of closing the book a quiet landing point.

03 / 执行Execution

从开本到分镜From format to panel

确定开本与网格后，依次完成封面主视觉绘制、中英文标准字与信息层级排布、环衬的图形延展，以及内页漫画分镜与「前言」页的图文编排。 Once format and grid were fixed, the work ran through cover art, the Chinese and Latin logotypes and their information hierarchy, the graphic extension onto endpapers, and then the interior comic panels and the illustrated preface spread.

内页沿用封面建立的黑白线稿语言，用统一的框线与留白节奏控制阅读速度。 Interiors continue the black-and-white line language set by the cover, using a consistent panel rule and rhythm of white space to pace the reading.

04 / 成果Outcome

一本可以拿在手里的书A book you can hold

交付封面、封底、环衬与内页版式的完整装帧方案，形成一本结构闭合、风格统一的成书。 A complete binding scheme — cover, back cover, endpapers and interior layout — forming a closed, internally consistent book.
